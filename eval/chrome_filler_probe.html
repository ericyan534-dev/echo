Echo — Chrome filler-stripping probe idle

Standalone proof of the v2 thesis: Chrome's Web Speech recognizer deletes "um/uh" and normalizes stretched vowels — no server, no Echo backend. Open in Chrome, click Start, read the script aloud, and screenshot the transcript. This is the artifact docs/ARCHITECTURE.md asks for.

1 · Read this aloud

Say, naturally with the fillers:
“Every morning I, um, make some, uh, toast.”
Then, stretching the vowel ~1 s:
“I’ll take theeee… train.”
What you’ll see on the right: the words come through, but the um / uh never appear, and theeee lands as a clean “the”. That gap is exactly what Echo’s acoustic channel exists to recover — the transcript literally cannot see the stall.
